# Code of conduct

Zearch is built in the open by people who care about evidence. We want this project to be a good place to work for everyone who takes part: in issues, pull requests, reviews and anywhere we represent the project.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Argue with evidence. Disagree about the work, not the person.
- Give and take review in good faith. Assume the other person is trying to help.
- Credit other people's work.
- Respect privacy. Don't share anyone's personal information, and don't post other people's private research or notes.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, threats, or demeaning remarks about anyone's identity or background
- Sexual language or imagery
- Deliberate disruption of discussions
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Pushing someone to keep engaging after they've asked you to stop

## Reporting

If something happens that makes you uncomfortable, tell the maintainers privately: message [@zearchcomputer](https://x.com/zearchcomputer) on X or contact [@brandononchain](https://github.com/brandononchain) on GitHub. We'll keep reports confidential and respond as quickly as we can.

## What happens next

Maintainers decide on a response in proportion to what happened: a private conversation, a warning, removal of content, or a temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces.

This code was written for Zearch and draws on the ideas in the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
